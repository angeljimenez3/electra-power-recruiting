## Key Observations for Recruiting Site

1. **Bilingual market confirmed** — Hispanic customers in South Florida, testimonials from Carlos Mendez and Maria Gonzalez
2. **Doral, FL office** — close to Miami, in heart of Hispanic business corridor
3. **Brand is purple-forward** — distinctive in solar (most competitors use blue/green/orange)
4. **No existing careers/recruiting page** — blank canvas opportunity
5. **Site built on Zyro** — basic builder, recruiting site should be a major step up
6. **Strong guarantee messaging** — can translate to employer brand ("we invest in our people like we invest in our systems")
7. **Community-first language** — "serving our community for generations" works for recruiting too
