## Patterns of the Top 10%

After analyzing all 10 competitors, here are the **5 patterns** that separate elite solar recruiting from average:

### Pattern 1: Lead with Money, Follow with Mission
Top performers put specific dollar figures in the headline. "$100k-$200k OTE" converts better than "competitive pay." Soligo puts compensation in the JOB TITLE. The mission (clean energy, helping families) comes second — it justifies the money but doesn't replace it.

### Pattern 2: Sell the Lifestyle, Not the Job
Soligo shows international trips, team events, weekly prizes. The best recruiters sell what the MONEY BUYS, not just the money itself. For Miami's young Hispanic market, this means: cars, lifestyle, nights out, travel, status.

### Pattern 3: Remove Every Barrier to Entry
"No experience required" + "paid training" + "we'll teach you everything." The top competitors make it feel risk-free to apply. The less friction, the more applicants.

### Pattern 4: Social Proof from Peers (Not Customers)
While solar customer testimonials matter for sales, recruiting sites need EMPLOYEE testimonials. Young people showing their earnings, their lifestyle, their growth. Video testimonials outperform text 10:1.

### Pattern 5: Mobile-First, Instant Apply
The target demo lives on their phone. The entire application flow must work perfectly on mobile in under 60 seconds. Long forms kill conversion.

## Recommended Design Direction (Based on Competitor Data)

### Colors
Keep Electra's purple (#673de6) as primary — it's distinctive in a sea of blue/green/orange solar companies. Add:
- Electric yellow/gold (#FFD700) as energy accent — signals money/success
- Deep dark (#0D0B1A) for premium dark sections
- Clean white for contrast

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, modern sans-serif (Poppins or Space Grotesk) — matches Soligo's proven approach
- **Body:** Clean, readable (Inter or DM Sans)
- **Accent:** Could include a Spanish-friendly display font for cultural connection

### Animation
- Scroll-triggered number counters (earnings, team size)
- Parallax lifestyle photography
- Smooth section transitions
- Card reveal animations for benefits/perks

### Structure
- Hero: Big earnings number + lifestyle imagery + instant apply CTA
- Social proof: Video testimonials from current setters
- Benefits: Visual cards with specific perks
- Culture: Team photos, events, Miami lifestyle
- Screening form: Smart, short, mobile-optimized
- Bilingual: Spanish as primary, English as secondary (flip the default)

### What to Avoid (Competitor Weaknesses)
- Generic job board templates (Icon Power, KR Solar)
- Corporate/boring tone (Sunrun)
- Walls of text in job descriptions
- Desktop-first design
- English-only content
- Hiding compensation details
